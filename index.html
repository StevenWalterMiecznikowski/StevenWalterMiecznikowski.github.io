About

My work as a fine arts painter is shaped by a personal journey of transformation, blending technical experience with creative exploration. I begin paintings without a fixed image in mind. What matters to me is the act of starting, of putting a mark down and paying attention to what it suggests next. Through accumulation, adjustment, and removal, forms emerge gradually from the material itself. Inspired by Joseph Beuys, I view art as a vehicle for healing and meaning. I am interested in the moment when an image almost resolves but does not fully settle, and in the ways looking can turn abstraction into recognition.
